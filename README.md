# math-concepts
Interactive, visual math puzzles that help young kids learn early math concepts through play.

## Puzzles

| Puzzle | What it teaches | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shape Balance](balance-scale/) | Balance-scale shape puzzles (early algebra): remove matching shapes from both sides, find what weighs the same, then balance a new scale. Random puzzles with Easy, Medium and Hard levels. | Open `balance-scale/index.html` in any browser |
| [Only One Way?](only-one-way/) | Fill-in-the-blank with <, = and > (e.g. 6 + __ < 9). Six steps: blocks, number-line hops, check every number, take-away towers, "one way or lots?" puzzles, and a Solve round where you fill the blank and decide whether to circle it. | Open `only-one-way/index.html` in any browser |
| [Mystery Numbers A & B](mystery-numbers/) | "I thought of two numbers": find A and B from their sum and difference. Scored 10-puzzle rounds with a worked explanation after each answer. | Open `mystery-numbers/index.html` in any browser |
| [Balance Weights](balance-weights/) | Find the weight of an object on a balanced scale (add up a side, take away, add then take away, two of the same). Scored 10-puzzle rounds with a worked explanation after each answer. | Open `balance-weights/index.html` in any browser |
